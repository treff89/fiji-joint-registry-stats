06 About the registry

What we record

Every total hip, hip hemiarthroplasty, total and partial knee replacement, and revision hip or knee operation in participating Fijian hospitals: the diagnosis, implants, fixation and any later revision.

Protecting patients

Patient records are held in a secure, access-controlled database — never on this website. Only approved, de-identified totals are published here. Counts from 1 to 4 are shown as “<5”, and surgeons are not named.

For hospitals

Hospital staff record operations through the secure registry application on a phone or computer. Contact the registry administrator for access.
